# Power Electronics Internship

This repository documents the technical work and learning carried out during my Power Electronics internship.

The work primarily focused on power converter design and simulation using LTspice, along with the study and analysis of GaN power devices and their datasheets.

## Areas Covered

### 1. Power Converters

The converter section contains LTspice simulation files and corresponding simulation results for:

- Boost Converter
- Power Factor Correction (PFC)
- Totem-Pole PFC

### 2. GaN Device Studies

The GaN section contains individual studies of GaN power devices.

Each device folder includes a presentation covering the device architecture, electrical characteristics, operating principles, and relevant datasheet parameters.

## Tools Used

- LTspice - Circuit Design and simulation
- Altium - Schematic Capture and PCB Design
- Microsoft PowerPoint - Technical Documentation
- Datasheets and technical documentation - Device Analysis

## Key Learning Outcome
- Understanding the operating principles of power converters
- Designing and simulating converter circuits using LTspice
- Analyzing voltage and current waveforms
- Understanding switching behavior in power converters
- Studying Power Factor Correction techniques
- Understanding the operation of Totem-Pole PFC architectures
- Studying GaN power devices and their electrical characteristics
- Interpreting semiconductor device datasheets
- Understanding the role of GaN devices in high-frequency power conversion
